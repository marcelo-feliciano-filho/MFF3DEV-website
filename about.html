About Marcelo

Built by an engineer, designed for enterprise outcomes.

My name is Marcelo Feliciano Filho, and I am the founder of MFF3DEV LLC. Over the past 7+ years building production backend systems, cloud platforms, and AI-powered applications for global teams, I have focused on one clear goal: delivering high-performance, resilient AI backend architecture and automation systems that companies can rely on in production.

My journey started from practical execution: solving production performance bottlenecks, scaling complex data pipelines, and transforming research-level AI algorithms into robust products. Today, I help engineering organizations and leadership teams build and scale systems using Python, Django, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Docker, AWS, and modern AI paradigms including RAG pipelines and Model Context Protocol (MCP) tooling.

Highlights include technical leadership of remote engineering teams on enterprise AI initiatives, high-throughput backend delivery across US and UK teams (BT, Turing), and ongoing doctoral research in Artificial Intelligence at PUCPR with publications at Springer LNCS.

I am available for W2 contracts, direct-hire remote engineering positions, and dedicated technical architecture engagements. For professional background, connect directly via: Marcelo’s LinkedIn profile or WhatsApp.
